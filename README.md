## What is Infinite Craft?

Infinite Craft is a popular browser-based game where players start with a few basic elements (like Water, Fire, Earth, and Air) and combine them to discover a vast universe of new items, concepts, and even fictional characters. Each new discovery is added to your collection, and the goal is to see what amazing and unexpected things you can create!

## How Does This Bookmarklet Hack Work?

This bookmarklet provides a simple way to enhance your Infinite Craft experience. It allows you to directly add custom elements to your local game. You can choose a name and an emoji for your new element, and it will be available for you to use in your combinations within your own browser.

[Go here to use it!](https://cloudwaddie.github.io/infinite-craft-hack/)

## Disclaimer

**Please read:** This tool is intended for educational purposes. It works by modifying data stored in your browser (specifically, `localStorage`) and does **not** interact with or alter the game's servers. This means your custom elements are only visible to you, and there's no risk of being banned from the game. Enjoy experimenting!
